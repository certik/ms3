    Exact or estimated?

    Exact Every mine layout that fits the revealed numbers and the total mine count is counted, and each cell shows the share of those layouts with a mine there.

    Estimated When the frontier is too tangled to count in time, weighted random layouts that fit the clues are sampled instead. These numbers carry a ~, can be substantially wrong and have no guaranteed error bound. Only the check and mine markers are certain.

    Flags are your notes. The solver does not treat them as mines and never looks at the hidden layout.

    How to play

    Open every cell that is not a mine. A number tells you how many of its 8 neighbors hide mines.

    Autosolve flags proven mines and opens proven-safe cells until a guess is needed. Choose your first cell, then pick among the remaining odds whenever it pauses. If you survive, autosolve continues. It never guesses or trusts sampled 0% / 100% estimates.

    • Click Enter Space Reveal a cell. On a number whose flags are all placed, open its other neighbors (chord).
    • Right-click F Flag or unflag a hidden cell.
    • Flag mode or long-press Flag cells on a touch screen.
    • Arrows Home End Move around the board. Ctrl+Home and Ctrl+End jump to the corners.

    Your first reveal is always safe. Mines are placed only after it and never in that cell or its neighbors, so the first click always opens an area.

    Each tab plays its own game, computed entirely in your browser. Reloading the page starts a new game; your difficulty, zoom, mine odds and autosolve settings are remembered.
